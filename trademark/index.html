Official Intellectual Property & Brand Protection Guidelines

1. Protected Marks & Assets

The names ItsRede™, Rede Records™, associated logos, branding graphics, custom avatars, audio assets, and original video content are protected intellectual property owned exclusively by ItsRede.

2. Unauthorized Use & Violations

You may not use the ItsRede name, logo, or associated branding to:

  • Impersonate ItsRede or Rede Records on any platform (YouTube, Roblox, Discord, Twitter/X, etc.).
  • Sell, re-upload, or distribute unauthorized merchandise, digital clothing (UGC), audio tracks, or code.
  • Mislead audiences into believing a project, server, channel, or video is officially endorsed or created by ItsRede.

3. Enforcement & Legal Consequences

Any unauthorized commercial use, copyright infringement, or trademark violation will result in immediate legal action and platform enforcement, including but not limited to:

  • Platform Action & Account Termination: Official DMCA takedown requests and trademark infringement notices submitted directly to hosting platforms (YouTube, Roblox, GitHub, Discord, hosting providers), which can lead to permanent account strikes, bans, or channel termination.
  • Cease and Desist Orders: Formal legal demand notices issued to halt infringing activities immediately.
  • Financial Liability & Fines: Civil litigation to recover statutory damages, financial losses, legal costs, and statutory fines allowed under applicable intellectual property laws.
